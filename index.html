Help & About

Privacy & Data Storage: everything you type into Daily Planner is saved only in this browser's local storage, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere - there is no account, no cloud copy and no tracking. If you clear this browser's site data, the planner is erased, so export a backup first. Opening the planner in a different browser or device starts a fresh, empty planner there; use Export and Import to move your data.

Welcome to Daily Planner

A calm place to plan each day: priorities, a simple schedule, habits and goals - all stored privately in your own browser, working fully offline.

Start with a clean planner, or explore first with sample days already filled in. You can load the sample data later from Settings, too.

Help & Tutorial

Getting started takes a minute:

  1. On the Today page, type a task in "Add a Task", pick a date, and press Add Task. Give it a time if you want it on the schedule.
  2. Tick the round checkbox when a task is done and watch the day's progress bar fill up.
  3. Tap the star on your most important tasks - up to three appear under Top Priorities.
  4. Set Repeat on a task (every day, weekdays, or weekly) and it will keep appearing on the right days by itself.
  5. Use the Prev / Next buttons or the date box to plan other days. Anything unfinished from earlier days shows under Overdue - one tap moves it to today.
  6. Add habits on the Habits page and tick them off through the week to build a streak.
  7. Track bigger aims on the Goals page, and keep loose thoughts on the Notes page.
  8. From Settings, export a backup file now and then. Your data lives only in this browser, so the backup is your safety copy.
